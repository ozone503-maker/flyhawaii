Fly Hawaii

Pilot a delivery saucer over the real Big Island. Pick up packages from local shops, drop them across the island and stack up electric blue Kudoken

Loading Big Island terrain
Keyboard W / S or arrows to fly, A / D to turn, Space to climb, Q / F to descend, Shift to boost, C to switch camera, T for time of day, any key or tap skips arrival scenes, M for music, , / . for previous and next track
Touch put your left thumb down anywhere on the left side and slide to fly and turn, right glyph pads to climb, descend and boost
Deliver hover low inside a beacon to beam packages up or down
Time trial a lap of the whole island through hex gates just off the coast, starting off Hilo Bay. Beat your best and race your ghost

Pickup and drop-off places are real Big Island businesses. Pilot and local portraits are original art made for Fly Hawaii. Map at true scale (1 unit = 100 m).
